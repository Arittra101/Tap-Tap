# Tap Tap — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

## The short version

**Tap Tap keeps everything on your phone.** There is no account, no sign-in, and no
server. Nothing you count, quit, or write is ever sent anywhere.

## What the app stores

Tap Tap saves the following on your device only:

- Your counters: names, notes, counts, targets, colours and backgrounds.
- Your quit counters: habit names, the date you stopped, money per day, goals and the
  reason you wrote.
- A log of what happened: taps, resets, urges beaten and slips. This is what "Today" and
  "urges beaten" are counted from.
- Your settings: colour theme, light/dark mode, language, vibration and sound.

This data lives in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it.

## What the app does not do

- **No account.** You never give us a name, an email address or a phone number.
- **No server.** The app works fully offline and never uploads your data.
- **No analytics about you.** We do not track what you count or how you use the app.
- **No ads.** There are none, and no advertising identifiers are collected.
- **No sharing or selling.** There is nobody to share with — the data never leaves
  your phone.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Only if you turn on the daily motivation message, so Tap Tap can send one short note each morning. You can refuse, and the app works the same otherwise. |
| Vibration | The short buzz when you tap, if you leave vibration on in Settings. |

Tap Tap asks for nothing else. No camera, no contacts, no location, no files.

## Backup

Android's own backup is on, so your counters can move to a new phone when you restore
it. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) — we never see it. You
can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a counter removes it and its history immediately. Uninstalling Tap Tap removes
everything. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing left to request or
erase.

## Children

Tap Tap collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and the date above
updated.
